# Nexa Learning Demo General FAQ

## About the platform
Nexa Learning Demo hosts self-paced online courses across programming, data
science, and web development. Most courses include video lessons, graded
quizzes, hands-on projects, and a completion certificate.

## Course access
Once purchased, individual courses are accessible for life, including all
future content updates. Subscription-plan courses remain accessible only
while the subscription is active.

## Prerequisites and difficulty
Course pages list prerequisites under "Before you start." Courses tagged
"Beginner" assume no prior experience; "Intermediate" courses assume
comfort with basic programming syntax; "Advanced" courses assume a
completed intermediate course in the same track.

## Offline access
The mobile app supports downloading video lessons for offline viewing.
Quizzes and code exercises still require an internet connection.

## Community and support
Every course has a linked discussion forum for students to ask questions
and share projects. General support (not covered by billing, technical,
or booking topics) can always be escalated to a human agent from the help
widget.

## Accessibility
All videos include auto-generated captions, editable by instructors for
accuracy. Students needing additional accommodations can request them from
Account Settings > Accessibility.

## Privacy
Personal data is never sold to third parties. Full details are in the
Privacy Policy linked in the site footer; data deletion requests are
processed within 30 days.
