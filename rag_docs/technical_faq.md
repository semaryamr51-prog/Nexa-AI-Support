# Nexa Learning Demo Technical Support FAQ

## Video playback issues
If videos fail to load or show a blank screen, first try switching to a
different browser (Chrome or Firefox, latest version). Video playback
requires JavaScript and cookies enabled. On mobile, ensure the app is
updated to the latest version from the App Store or Play Store.

## Login and password reset
Password reset emails can take up to 10 minutes to arrive and are sometimes
filtered to spam. If no email arrives after 15 minutes, the account email
may be mistyped — contact support to verify the email on file.

## Progress not saving
Course progress saves automatically every 30 seconds while a lesson is
open. If progress isn't saving, it's usually a lost connection; the app
caches progress locally and syncs once connectivity returns, so don't
re-do the lesson immediately — wait a few minutes and refresh.

## Known browser/device issues
Safari on older macOS versions (pre-Ventura) can show a blank code editor
due to an outdated WebKit build; updating macOS resolves this. The Android
app has a known issue where dark mode resets after a phone restart; a fix
is scheduled for the next release.

## Certificates
Certificates generate automatically within 24 hours of completing 100% of
a course's required modules (graded quizzes included, optional readings
excluded). If 24 hours pass with no certificate, the most common cause is
one incomplete graded quiz.

## Two-factor authentication
2FA codes are sent via the method chosen at signup (SMS or authenticator
app). If codes aren't arriving via SMS, check that international SMS is
enabled with your carrier, or switch to an authenticator app in Account
Settings > Security.
