# Nexa Learning Demo Booking & Office Hours Policy

## Office hours
Instructors hold weekly office hours (30-minute slots) that can be booked
up to 7 days in advance and no later than 2 hours before the slot. Each
student may book at most 2 slots per week per course.

## Mentoring sessions
1:1 mentoring sessions are available to Pro and Team plan subscribers,
scheduled in 30- or 60-minute blocks. Free-tier users can join group
mentoring sessions instead.

## Rescheduling and cancellations
Sessions can be rescheduled or cancelled up to 4 hours before the start
time with no penalty. Cancelling with less than 4 hours' notice counts
against your monthly session quota.

## Live workshops and cohort calls
Live workshops require registration at least 24 hours in advance because
seats are capped. Cohort kickoff calls are open to everyone enrolled in
that cohort and don't require separate booking.

## Make-up sessions
If you miss a booked session without cancelling, one make-up session per
month is offered free of charge; additional missed sessions may incur a
small rebooking fee depending on your plan.
