# Nexa Learning Demo Billing FAQ

## Refund policy
Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase if you have completed less
than 20% of a course. After 20% completion, refunds are only granted for
duplicate charges or verified technical failures that prevented access.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5–10 business
days to appear.

## Subscription billing cycle
Subscriptions renew monthly on the same calendar day you first subscribed.
You can cancel any time from Account Settings > Billing; cancelling stops
future renewals but does not refund the current period.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. Installment plans
(3 or 6 months, 0% interest) are available for individual course purchases
over $150, but not for monthly subscriptions.

## Duplicate charges
Duplicate charges are almost always caused by a browser double-submitting
the checkout form. If you see two charges for the same order within a few
minutes of each other, the second charge is automatically refunded within
3 business days — no ticket needed, but you can request expedited review.

## Student discounts
Verified students get 40% off individual courses (not subscriptions) with a
valid .edu email or uploaded student ID. Discounts cannot be applied
retroactively to past purchases.

## Invoices and receipts
Every purchase generates an invoice under Account > Billing > Invoices.
Company reimbursement invoices (with tax ID fields) can be requested from
the same page.
